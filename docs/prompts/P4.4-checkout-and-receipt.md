## Goal

A guest places a walk-in, pickup or delivery order, pays cash or uploads a GCash/Maya/bank proof, and follows the order live on a receipt page they can keep.

## Read first

`CLAUDE.md`, `docs/ux-plan.md` sections 3, 5.5 to 5.7 and 7, `docs/api-surface.md` slice 3 (`useQuote`, `usePlaceOrder`, `useOrder`, `useOrderLive`, `uploadPaymentProof`, `useSubmitPaymentProof`, `useMyOrderTokens`, error codes), the P4.0 patterns. Plan first; stop and say what is missing if a hook isn't there.

## Build only

1. **Checkout** (`/[shop]/checkout`), one page, three sections (no stepper), React Hook Form + `OrderPayload` from `packages/core`:
   - How: `toggle-group` of the order types the shop has on. Pickup: "As soon as possible" or a slot today or tomorrow inside business hours (`native-select`, 15-minute slots from `packages/core/src/time.ts`). Delivery: address, landmark, zone with its fee, or "Fee confirmed on Messenger".
   - Who: name, mobile (`PhoneField`), email optional, "Remember me on this phone" (on by default, local storage, try/catch).
   - Pay: `radio-group` cards of the methods allowed for the chosen type only. Transfer methods show account name and number with `CopyButton`, the QR (tap to enlarge) and "Send exactly ₱345".
   - Totals from `useQuote` (re-run when the type, zone or cart changes); problems shown on their line with Remove; Place order disabled while there are problems.
   - Privacy checkbox (paper 10) and `StickyActionBar` "Place order · ₱345". Idempotent client key per attempt; spinner and disabled while sending; offline disables it with "You're offline".
   - Shop-level errors (`shop_closed`, `shop_busy`, `fulfillment_unavailable`, `cod_not_allowed`, …) show `errorWords(code, vocab)` above the button.
   - Success: clear the cart, keep the token through `useMyOrderTokens`, go to the receipt.
2. **Receipt** (`/[shop]/o/[token]`), data from `useOrder(token)` + `useOrderLive(token)` (Realtime broadcast, 15 s poll fallback):
   - First view after placing: the "Order placed!" moment, `#042` big, and the type's line ("Show this at the counter", "Pickup 3:30 PM", "We'll message you when it's on the way").
   - QR of this page (`qr-code`), horizontal `OrderTimeline`, `PaymentBadge`.
   - Transfer not yet paid: an amber "Upload proof" card (`PhotoField` → `uploadPaymentProof`, reference number, `useSubmitPaymentProof`). Pending: "Checking your payment". Rejected: red alert with the reason and upload again. `duplicate_reference` and `too_many_proofs`: their words from `errorWords`.
   - `OrderLines`, total, payment method; "Send order to our Messenger" (m.me link with the paper 6.6 text from a core function), "Copy order details" (same text), "Save receipt" (`navigator.share` with fallback to print).
   - Live: status changes update the timeline without a reload.
3. **My orders** (`/[shop]/orders`): orders from `useMyOrderTokens(slug)`, newest first, `item` rows with number, date (Manila), total, `OrderStatusBadge`; empty state links to the menu.

## Done when (I will test on a phone with Juan's Cafe and Tala Apparel)

- Walk-in, cash: placed, receipt shows `#00N`, QR and "Placed".
- Pickup, GCash: I copy the number, place, upload a proof with a reference; the receipt shows "Checking your payment". A second order with the same reference is blocked with a clear message.
- Delivery with a zone fee: the total includes the fee; cash on delivery appears only when the shop allows it.
- Double-tapping Place order makes one order. Turning off data before tapping shows "You're offline".
- When the owner changes the status in the database (or in P4.5), the receipt timeline updates without a reload.
- "Send order to our Messenger" opens Messenger with the order text.
- 390 px with no sideways scroll, 0 console errors. `pnpm check` is green.
