## Goal

The owner opens the dashboard and sees, in one look, what needs them now and how today is going; they can find any customer and see the last 30 days of sales.

## Read first

`CLAUDE.md`, `docs/ux-plan.md` sections 2 (what went wrong with Home last time), 5.14 to 5.16 and 7, the hooks from P5.6, the P4.0 patterns. Plan first; stop and say what is missing.

## Build only

1. **Home** (`/`), only these parts (paper 9.4):
   - Greeting by Manila time and `ShareShop` (link, QR download, "Share on Messenger").
   - Setup checklist (logo, hours, payment account, first product, order types) with `progress`; each row links to its screen; hidden when all are done.
   - Attention chips: "2 to verify", "3 waiting", "1 low stock", each linking to the filtered list; hidden at 0.
   - Four `stat` cards: Sales today (number ticker), Orders (with "3 open"), Average order, New members this week. No comparisons, no ranges.
   - Members card (member green): count and top 3 suki this month.
   - In progress: up to 5 compact `OrderCard` + "View all on Orders" (same buttons as the board, from `nextActions`). Best sellers today: top 5.
   - `QueryState` per card; a fresh day with no orders reads calmly ("No orders yet today").
2. **Customers** (`customers`): ReUI `data-grid` on laptop (name, mobile, Member badge, orders, total spent, last order; search; "Members only"; CSV), `item` rows on phones. Row → `ResponsiveDialog` with their orders (compact `OrderCard`) and `StampCard`.
3. **Reports** (`reports`): `toggle-group` Today / 7 days / 30 days; `stat` totals; sales bars per day (per hour for Today) with `chart`, lazy-loaded; best sellers table; "Download CSV" of orders. Manila dates throughout.

## Do not build

- % vs last week, date-range pickers, member sign-up charts, shortcut tiles, payment-mix donut, stale-order cleanup, scheduled orders, Google Sheets (all roadmap; see `docs/ux-plan.md` 9).
