## Goal

## Definitions (write them as comments above each function)

- Business date: the order's `business_date` (Manila).
- **Sales**: sum of `total` of orders with `payment_status = 'paid'` and status not `cancelled` or `expired`.
- **Orders**: count of orders not `expired` (cancelled ones count as orders but not as sales).
- **Open orders**: `placed`, `preparing`, `ready`, `out_for_delivery`. **Waiting**: `placed`. **To verify**:
  `payment_status = 'pending'`. **Low stock**: tracked variants not archived with `stock_on_hand <= low_stock_at`.
- **Average order**: sales ÷ paid orders (0 when none), rounded to whole centavos.
- **Best sellers**: by quantity from `order_items` of sales orders, top 5, grouped by `item_name`.
- **Top suki**: the three members with the highest sales this Manila month.
- **New members this week**: `member_since` in the last 7 days.

### 2. `demo_reset` (Appendix B)

Migration `<timestamp>_demo_reset.sql`: `private.demo_reset(p_slug text) returns jsonb`, execute granted to
nobody (SQL editor and `seed.sql` only). Refuses unless `is_demo` (`not_demo`). It:
- deletes only demo rows of that shop: orders whose token starts with `demo-` (and their lines, events, payments,
  ledger rows), customers tagged `demo`, their addresses and consents; resets the shop's `order_counters`;
- creates about 120 customers (about 75 members, `member_since` spread over 90 days, `user_id` null; Filipino names,
  `+639` numbers from a reserved fake block such as `+63999000xxxx`), all tagged `demo`;
- creates 30 days of orders relative to `now()` with an upward trend (more on Fridays and weekends, lunch and
  afternoon peaks), all three fulfillments and every payment method the shop allows, a few cancelled with reasons
  and a few expired; numbers gap-free per day; tokens `demo-<random>`;
- today: 3 in progress (one preparing, one ready, one out for delivery for a delivery shop), 1 with a pending GCash
  proof (`proof_path` points to a path that does not exist; the proof viewer shows "Proof image not found"), 2
  pickups for tomorrow;
- stamps: earned rows for completed orders of members, a few redeemed, so some members have a reward ready;
- low stock on 2 variants;
- returns counts `{ customers, members, orders, today }`.
Prices, totals and lines must satisfy every check constraint; build them from the shop's real catalog.
`private.demo_setup()` now also calls `demo_reset` for both shops; `seed.sql` stays one line.

Add to `supabase/snippets/founder.sql`: "Demo: refresh both shops for today"
(`select private.demo_reset('juans-cafe'); select private.demo_reset('tala-apparel');`).

### 3. `packages/api` slice 6

Every name in `docs/api-surface.md` slice 6.
- `useCustomers(tenantId, { search, membersOnly })`: customers with `customer_stamp_balances`, search on name or
  phone (`ilike`, escaped), newest order first, 50 per page with "Load more".
- `exportOrdersCsv(client, tenantId, { from, to })`: one row per order (number, Manila date and time, type,
  customer, items as text, subtotal, discount, delivery fee, total in pesos with two decimals, payment method and
  status, order status). The CSV text comes from `toCsv(rows)` in `packages/core` (quotes, commas, newlines, a
  leading `'` on cells starting with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` so spreadsheets never run them as formulas).
