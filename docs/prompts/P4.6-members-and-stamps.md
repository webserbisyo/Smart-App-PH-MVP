## Goal

A guest joins the shop's stamp card right from the receipt with an email code, sees stamps grow with each completed order, uses the reward at checkout, and installs the shop as an app.

## Read first

`CLAUDE.md`, `docs/ux-plan.md` sections 3.2, 5.4, 5.6 to 5.8, 5.13 (stamp card) and 6, `docs/api-surface.md` slice 5, the P3b customer sign-in, the P4.0 `StampCard`. Plan first; stop and say what is missing.

## Build only

1. **Stamp card settings** (dashboard `settings/stamps`): stamps needed, minimum order (`PesoField`), reward (₱ off, shown as "₱100 off your order"), "Show members count on my shop" switch, live `StampCard` preview (it starts with the 1 welcome stamp every new member gets, decision P4-F4; not a setting). Same `SettingsSection` pattern as P4.2.
2. **Join sheet** (storefront `ResponsiveDialog`, opened from the receipt, the account page and the cart): the shop's rule in one line plus "Join now and get your first stamp free", email → 6-digit code (reuse the P3b sign-in parts), then `useJoinAsMember({ slug, name, phone, orderToken })` with a token from `useMyOrderTokens(slug)` (that is what links past guest orders; a phone alone could claim a stranger's stamps). No token on this phone → the server answers `phone_needs_order`: show "Place your first order, then join from the receipt". Success shows the `StampCard` with the welcome stamp (given by P5.5's join, shown as "Welcome stamp" in the history) plus the linked stamps, and "This order counts once it's completed".
3. **Receipt**: the "Join to earn stamps · this order counts" card for guests; members see "+1 stamp when completed".
4. **Cart and checkout**: members see the stamp line from `stampProgress(balance, rule)` ("2 more orders to a free drink"); when the reward is ready, a switch "Use ₱100 off" that `quote_order` applies. Checkout is prefilled from the account and the saved address.
5. **Account** (`/[shop]/account`): signed out: explainer + Join. Signed in: `StampCard` (confetti once when the reward becomes ready, remembered per device), my orders from the account (any device), saved address (edit), Sign out.
6. **Install guide**: after the first order on the receipt, and on the account page: "Add <shop> to your home screen". Android/desktop Chrome: the `beforeinstallprompt` event. iPhone Safari: a `ResponsiveDialog` with 3 drawn steps (Share, Add to Home Screen, Add). Hidden when already installed (`display-mode: standalone`) and for 14 days after "Not now" (local storage, try/catch). The per-shop manifest and icons come in P6; until then the install uses the current manifest.

## Do not build

- Points, tiers, referral codes, birthday rewards, push notifications, announcements, member export (roadmap).

## Rules

- `pnpm check` green. Test the install-prompt decision (pure function of platform, standalone, last dismissed) and the stamp line words.
- No new packages. No `any`, no casts, no `@ts-ignore`. Never read or print `.env.local`.

## Done when (I will test on a phone with Juan's Cafe and Tala Apparel)

- As a guest I place an order, tap Join on the receipt, enter the email code, and see my stamp card with 1 welcome stamp already filled and my earlier orders on the same mobile linked.
- Joining twice (or on a second phone) never gives a second welcome stamp.
- When the owner completes my paid order, the stamp appears; when they cancel it, the stamp goes away.
- At the target, the cart offers the reward; using it lowers the total and the next card starts again.
- Members count shows on the shop home only when the owner turns it on.
- On an iPhone the install steps show; on Android the install prompt opens; neither shows again after "Not now".
- 390 px with no sideways scroll, 0 console errors. `pnpm check` is green.
