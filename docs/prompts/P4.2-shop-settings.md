## Goal

An owner sets everything the storefront and checkout need: look, contact, hours, order types and delivery zones, and payment accounts, each section saved on its own.

## Read first

`CLAUDE.md`, `docs/ux-plan.md` sections 3.4, 5.13 and 7, the settings hooks P5.1 added, the P4.0 patterns, and `packages/ui/src/shop-theme.tsx` and `packages/core/src/color.ts`. Plan first; if a hook is missing, stop and say which.

## Build only

In `apps/dashboard/src/features/settings/`:

1. **Settings home** (`settings`): `item` rows with an icon, the section name and a one-line summary ("Open today 7 AM to 9 PM", "GCash, Maya"). Each opens its page.
2. **Shop look** (`settings/look`): logo (`PhotoField`, 512 px square), cover (1600 px, 16:9), name, short description, colours 60/30/10 with 4 presets (Uling, Kape, Ube, Dagat) and `color-picker`, a contrast warning below 4.5:1 using `color.ts`, and a live phone preview (scaled `ShopHeader`, category chips and one `ProductCard` inside `ShopTheme`). On desktop the preview sits beside the form; on phones under it.
3. **Business info** (`settings/info`): contact number (`PhoneField`), address, Facebook page, Messenger username with "Test link" (opens m.me/username).
4. **Hours** (`settings/hours`): 7 day rows (open switch, from, to, Manila time), closed days (`calendar`, multiple dates), "Pause orders now" switch with an optional reason.
5. **Order types** (`settings/order-types`): Walk-in, Pickup, Delivery switches; pay-later rules (cash at the counter, pay on pickup, cash on delivery); delivery zones (name + `PesoField` fee, `sortable`) or "Fee confirmed on Messenger". At least one type must stay on.
6. **Payment accounts** (`settings/payments`): rows for GCash, Maya and bank (`sortable`), each with account name, number and QR (`PhotoField`), in a `ResponsiveDialog` editor.

Each page is a `SettingsSection` with its own Save in a `StickyActionBar`, React Hook Form + the Zod schemas from `packages/core`, "Saved" toast, unsaved-changes guard, `QueryState`. Slug, status and paid_until are not editable (decision P3-2): show the shop link read-only with `CopyButton`.
